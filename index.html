Gold Watch
Finance · Trading

A research platform for XAU/USD traders. Gold Watch consolidates fundamental and technical analysis from across the web into a single daily briefing — market sentiment, news, swing highs and lows, and open/close signals. Built for traders who don't have time to read ten different sources before the market opens.

A private management platform for a residential property development in Spain. Homeowners log in to report maintenance issues and track the overall status of the development they've invested in. The management team uses it to oversee budgets, contractors, landscaping, pool maintenance, and long-term forecasting — all in one place.

Ownership & Payment

Your money is protected.
Your code is yours.

Every product we build is yours. Once the project is complete, you own the code outright — no licensing fees, no lock-in, no ongoing charges from us.

Payments are held in escrow via Trullo — a neutral third party that releases funds to us only once the agreed deliverables have been met. Your money is protected throughout the build.
